/*
 * Simple single‑channel oscilloscope front end for ESP32
 *
 *             +-------+----- D1 TVS (optional) -----+
 *             |                                     |
 * IN ---R1---o+                                     |
 *             |          SW1       +-- R2 --o       |
 *             +----o----o/ o-------+         |      |
 *                  |    1× path    |         +-- R3-+-- GND
 *                  |               |
 *                  +-- 10× path ---+
 *
 * After the switch, the signal is AC‑coupled through C1 into a mid‑bias node formed by R4 and R5:
 *
 *                R4 (100 kΩ)
 *  +3.3 V ---/\/\/\/\---+
 *                     |
 *             C1      +----> to op‑amp input
 *  Signal ---||-------+
 *                     |
 *                 R5 (100 kΩ)
 *                     |
 *                    GND
 *
 * The biased signal is buffered by one channel of a dual rail‑to‑rail op‑amp (U1A) configured as a voltage follower.
 * The op‑amp output drives a simple RC low‑pass filter (Rf/Cf) before reaching the ESP32 ADC pin.
 *
 *          +3.3 V (supply)
 *           |
 *         +---+
 *         | U1|
 *         +---+--+--------o---- Rf 100 Ω -----> ADC1_CH6 (GPIO34)
 *                |
 *                |                 |
 *                +-----------------+ Cf 1 nF
 *                                 |
 *                                GND
 *
 * The second channel of the op‑amp (U1B) is unused and should be configured as a voltage follower with its input tied to a stable node (e.g. ground) to prevent oscillation.
 */
